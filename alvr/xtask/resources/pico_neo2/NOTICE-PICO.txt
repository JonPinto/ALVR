PICO Neo 2 SteamVR resources
============================

The files in this directory (render models, textures, status icons, input
profiles and binding descriptors) originate from the PICO Link / PICO Streaming
Assistant SteamVR driver, which is proprietary software distributed by PICO
(ByteDance).

They are included for interoperability only: ALVR emulates a PICO Neo 2 headset
and controllers, and SteamVR can only render them if these resources are
available to the driver. The assets are unmodified except for the `{pico}` ->
`{alvr_server}` path rewrites in `input/pico_neo2_controller_profile.json` and
`input/pico_neo2_hmd_profile.json`, the vendor prefix in those two files, and
the removal of two textures per controller that no material references.

ALVR's MIT license does not apply to these assets. No license, express or
implied, is granted to them, and all rights remain with PICO.

If you are a rights holder and want these files removed, please open an issue.
